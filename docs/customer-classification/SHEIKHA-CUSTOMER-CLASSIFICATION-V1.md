# SHEIKHA_CUSTOMER_CLASSIFICATION_V1

## Purpose

SHEIKHA_CUSTOMER_CLASSIFICATION_V1 turns Sheikha customer, merchant, company, government, provider, investor, and individual classification into governed runtime data.

It is used by registration, dashboards, contracts, licensing, billing, permissions, commercial priority, and sales operations.

## Classification Flow

1. Customer Segment
2. Customer Type
3. Business Model
4. Role
5. Verification Level
6. Commercial Priority
7. Sector
8. Dashboard Route
9. Contract Gate
10. Billing Gate

## Runtime Routes

- `/api/customers/classification/status`
- `/api/customers/classification/taxonomy`
- `/api/customers/classification/segments`
- `/api/customers/classification/routing`

## Truthfulness Gate

Classification is not a signed contract, customer acquisition proof, partner proof, or revenue proof. Government, strategic account, supplier approval, partner status, and revenue status require their own evidence.
