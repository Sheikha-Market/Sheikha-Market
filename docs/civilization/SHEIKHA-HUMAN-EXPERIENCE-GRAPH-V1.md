# SHEIKHA HUMAN EXPERIENCE GRAPH V1

## Status

`SHEIKHA_HUMAN_EXPERIENCE_GRAPH_V1` is active as a governed graph layer that
links human experience to domains, governance controls, and impact metrics.

## Purpose

The graph converts human observations, field operations, project experiences,
decisions, lessons learned, research notes, incidents, quality findings, and
innovation hypotheses into reusable knowledge for learning and decision support.

## Graph Structure

- Experience nodes
- Domain nodes
- Governance control nodes
- Impact metric nodes

Primary relations:

- `CLASSIFIED_AS`
- `GOVERNED_BY`
- `MEASURED_BY`

## Governance

- Experiences are human-provided.
- Consent or authority is required.
- Sensitive data is not captured by default.
- High-impact use requires human approval.
- Sensitive domains require qualified review.
- Religious rulings are not automated.
- Audit is required.

## Boundaries

The graph does not claim machine consciousness, self-awareness, AGI, ASI,
infallibility, or unseen knowledge. It is a governed tool for organizing human
experience and improving beneficial work.
