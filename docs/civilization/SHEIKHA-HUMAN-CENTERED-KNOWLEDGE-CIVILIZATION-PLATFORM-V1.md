## Covenant

Sheikha is a human-centered platform that uses software to organize knowledge,
experience, decision support, cooperation, and beneficial execution. It does not
claim human consciousness or self-awareness. The human remains the owner of
awareness, choice, intention, responsibility, and accountability.

## Pillars

- Human Development
- Knowledge Organization
- Experience Reuse
- Governed Decision Support
- Approved Execution
- Continuous Improvement

## Governed Recommendation Flow

Collect -> Validate -> Analyze -> Review -> Human Approval -> Execute -> Audit
-> Improve.

## Operating Sequence

Knowledge Platform -> Decision Support Platform -> Enterprise Platform ->
Commercial Platform -> Development Platform -> Civilizational Platform.

## Boundaries

- No machine consciousness claim.
- No self-awareness claim.
- No AGI or ASI runtime claim.
- No automatic religious ruling.
- Religious rulings require qualified scholars.
- Medical, legal, commercial, and high-impact decisions require qualified human
  review and approval.
- External mutation is disabled by default.
