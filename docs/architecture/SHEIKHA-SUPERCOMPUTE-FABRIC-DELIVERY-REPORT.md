## Governance

The fabric is governed by Quran and Sunnah principles as an operational safety
policy. It does not claim consciousness, unseen knowledge, or independent
authority. Human review remains required before impact, publish, deployment, or
destructive action.

The compute and storage policy blocks:

- riba
- fraud
- harm and darar
- intoxicants
- mufattir content
- haram workloads
- haram storage
- private data exposure

## Acceptance Criteria

- Supercompute status returns `SHEIKHA_DATACENTER_SUPERCOMPUTE_FABRIC`.
- Distributed status returns `SHEIKHA_DISTRIBUTED_CLUSTER_FABRIC`.
- Final delivery returns `SHEIKHA_SUPERCOMPUTE_FABRIC_FINAL_DELIVERY`.
- Storage governance remains active and halal/no-harm only.
- External providers remain isolated and replaceable.
- Feature expansion is frozen during canonical stabilization.
