## ⚠️ Troubleshooting Guide

**Problem**: Email not delivered (bounced)
- **Solution**: Check email address spelling, resend to corrected address
- **Action**: Log in tracking sheet, note bounce reason

**Problem**: No response after 48 hours
- **Solution**: This is normal. Proceed with follow-up plan (Nov 24+)
- **Action**: Track in dashboard, prepare follow-up email

**Problem**: Negative response ("Not interested")
- **Solution**: Ask for 15-min call to understand concerns, offer to schedule later
- **Action**: Document response, escalate to Founder if critical

**Problem**: Question asked but can't answer
- **Solution**: Draft answer in PARTNER-OUTREACH-EXECUTION.md, Appendix B
- **Action**: Send response within 24 hours, copy Founder on reply

**Problem**: Email looks unprofessional (formatting issue)
- **Solution**: Send apology + corrected version immediately
- **Action**: Note in tracking, resend with apology
