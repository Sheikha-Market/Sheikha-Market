# SHEIKHA_EVIDENCE_REGISTRY_V1

Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Central evidence registry connecting Master Log, decisions, audits, knowledge, and impact.

## Lifecycle

Event → Log → Evidence → Decision → Audit → Impact → Knowledge

## Governance

- Evidence before decision
- Governance before execution
- No secret exposure
- Human approval for high risk
- No claim of unseen knowledge
- No automated religious final judgment

## Missing Evidence Research

`POST /api/sheikha/evidence-registry/research-plan` creates a governed research plan. It searches registered local references first, records candidate provenance and hadith-isnad requirements, and identifies authorized external channels that still need a connector or human reviewer.

A candidate source never becomes `VERIFIED` merely because it was found. Quran text location and integrity, hadith narrator/source/number/grade, scholarly interpretation, rational inference, and empirical measurement remain separate evidence layers. Religious interpretation requires a qualified scholar; domain claims require the relevant expert.
