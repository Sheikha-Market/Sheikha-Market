# SHEIKHA_PLATFORM_FACTORIES_V1

## Purpose

SHEIKHA_PLATFORM_FACTORIES_V1 activates four governed factories above the customer revenue experience layer:

- Science Factory
- Education Factory
- Product Factory
- Impact Factory

The layer converts knowledge, customer needs, product readiness, and revenue experience into reviewed outputs, learning paths, product packages, and measured impact.

## Runtime Routes

- `/api/platform/factories/status`
- `/api/platform/factories/factories`
- `/api/platform/factories/pipeline`

## Governance Flow

Knowledge Capture -> Evidence Review -> Factory Assignment -> Output Generation -> Quality Gate -> Customer or Internal Review -> Impact Measurement -> Decision Registry Update.

## Truthfulness Gate

This layer does not claim accreditation, public scientific publication, product launch, impact, or revenue without the required evidence, review, contract, measurement, or payment.
