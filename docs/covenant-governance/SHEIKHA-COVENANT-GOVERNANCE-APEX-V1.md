# SHEIKHA Covenant Governance Apex V1

This is the highest internal governance assurance layer and promotion gate. It is a
software governance tool under the Master Life Cycle reference and Civilization
Governance authority. It is not a religious, sovereign, governmental, or regulatory
authority.

## Numbered policy map

- `SGA-001`: truth and evidence before claim, mapped to `QURAN-17-036` and `QURAN-49-006`.
- `SGA-002`: trust and rightful duty, mapped to `QURAN-04-058`.
- `SGA-003`: justice and impartiality, mapped to `QURAN-05-008` and `QURAN-16-090`.
- `SGA-004`: no harm and reversibility, mapped to `SUNNAH-NAWAWI-032`.
- `SGA-005`: intention and accountability, mapped to `SUNNAH-BUKHARI-001`.
- `SGA-006`: governed method and continuity, mapped to `SUNNAH-ABU-DAWUD-4607`.

These are reference locators, not automated interpretations. Hadith numbering can vary
by edition. Religious interpretation and application require qualified human scholarly
review; technical and legal controls require their own independent specialist evidence.

## Promotion contract

A layer can be promoted only when tests, lint, governance, and `NO_DRIFT` pass; evidence
is linked; rollback is ready; authority claims are verified; required specialist review
is complete; and an authorized human explicitly approves the promotion.

The gate records promotion evidence but does not execute deployment, issue religious
rulings, or claim external authority.
