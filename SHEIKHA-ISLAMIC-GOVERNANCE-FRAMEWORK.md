## 📜 Core Islamic Foundation

### **Tawheed (التوحيد) - Unity with Allah**

The supreme principle of all governance decisions:
- **Arabic**: لا إله إلا الله محمد رسول الله
- **Translation**: There is no deity except Allah, and Muhammad is His Messenger
- **In Practice**: Every system decision glorifies Allah and follows His guidance

### **Islamic Pillars (أركان الإسلام)**

1. **Shahada** - Declaration of Faith (belief in Allah's oneness)
2. **Salah** - Prayer (spiritual connection and mindfulness)
3. **Zakat** - Charity (fair distribution of wealth)
4. **Sawm** - Fasting (discipline and self-control)
5. **Hajj** - Pilgrimage (equality and unity)

## ⚖️ GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

### **1. Shura (الشورى) - Consultation**

**Quranic Basis**: 
- Quran 42:38: "And those who have responded to their lord and established prayer and whose affair is [determined by] consultation among themselves"

**Implementation**:
- All major decisions made through consultation
- Multiple stakeholders involved
- Consensus-seeking approach
- Transparent deliberation process

### **2. Adalah (العدل) - Justice**

**Quranic Basis**:
- Quran 5:8: "O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses for Allah"

**Implementation**:
- Fair treatment for all regardless of status
- Impartial resource allocation
- Equal opportunity for all
- Transparent dispute resolution
- No favoritism or discrimination

### **3. Amanah (الأمانة) - Trustworthiness & Accountability**

**Quranic Basis**:
- Quran 4:58: "Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to their rightful owners"

**Implementation**:
- Full accountability for all actions
- Transparent reporting and documentation
- Public accountability before stakeholders
- Regular audits and reviews
- Clear responsibility assignment

### **4. Rahmah (الرحمة) - Mercy & Compassion**

**Prophetic Guidance**:
- Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: "Whoever does not show mercy will not be shown mercy"

**Implementation**:
- Compassionate treatment of all
- Care for vulnerable members
- Support in times of difficulty
- Forgiveness and second chances
- Human dignity always respected

### **5. Hikmah (الحكمة) - Wisdom**

**Quranic Basis**:
- Quran 2:269: "He gives wisdom to whom He wills, and whoever has been given wisdom has certainly been given much good"

**Implementation**:
- Wise decision-making based on knowledge
- Long-term vision and planning
- Learning from experience
- Consultation of experts
- Thoughtful consideration before action

## 🕌 MAQASID SHARIA (مقاصد الشريعة)
### Five Objectives of Islamic Law

Every governance decision must protect and promote:

1. **Deen (الدين) - Religion**
   - Protect freedom of belief and practice
   - Support spiritual development
   - Preserve Islamic values
   - Enable worship and prayer

2. **Nafs (النفس) - Life**
   - Protect physical safety and security
   - Ensure health and wellbeing
   - Support quality of life
   - Prevent harm and injury

3. **Aql (العقل) - Intellect**
   - Support education and learning
   - Promote critical thinking
   - Encourage knowledge seeking
   - Protect mental health

4. **Nasl (النسل) - Family & Progeny**
   - Strengthen family bonds
   - Protect children and youth
   - Support marriage and kinship
   - Ensure generational continuity

5. **Maal (المال) - Wealth**
   - Enable just and fair wealth accumulation
   - Prevent theft and fraud
   - Ensure fair wages and contracts
   - Support economic justice
